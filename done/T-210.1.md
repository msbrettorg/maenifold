Answered all 9 research questions:
1. **WebSocket URL**: `wss://go-msit.trouter.teams.microsoft.com/v4/c?tc={json}&epid={uuid}&timeout=40&...`
2. **Auth flow**: Bearer token for `https://ic3.teams.office.com` via `5:::{"name":"user.authenticate",...}`
3. **Registration**: POST to `https://teams.cloud.microsoft/registrar/prod/V2/registrations` with TTL=3600s, re-register every 45 min
4. **Frame format**: Socket.IO-style (`1::` connect, `5:::` events, `3:::` messages, `6:::` acks)
5. **Message structure**: EventMessage with resourceType="NewMessage", resource containing content/from/conversationLink
6. **Heartbeat**: 30s ping/pong via `5:N+::{"name":"ping"}` / `6:::N+["pong"]`
7. **Reconnection**: Exponential backoff 5s→300s, token refresh at 50 min
8. **HTML stripping**: Regex `<[^>]+>` + html.unescape
9. **Keyword matching**: Word-boundary regex + Teams `<at>` tag fallback

Crate recommendations: tokio-tungstenite, reqwest, serde, tokio, regex, base64, azure_identity
