# T-238.31c — Wire Schedule workdir and timeout_seconds from tool payload

## Problem

Schedule `workdir` and `timeout_seconds` fields exist on ScheduledTask struct and are respected by the supervisor, but the tool entrypoint hardcodes them (workdir: None, timeout: 300) instead of reading from the payload.

## Fix

Parse `workdir` and `timeout_seconds` from payload in `create_task` and `update_task`.

## Files

- `crates/maenifold-core/src/tools/schedule.rs`

## Verification

- `cargo test -p maenifold-core -- schedule`
- `cargo build --release -p maenifold-core`
