# T-179.31 — Hero vertical anchoring

## Intent

Both Home and Pipelines heroes drop content too low (~38% from top). Cowork anchors at ~28-32%. Too much dead space above.

## Scope

Files:
- apps/desktop/src/components/HomeDashboard.vue
- apps/desktop/src/components/TemplateGallery.vue

## Changes

Replace justify-center with padding-top approach:
- Remove justify-center from the hero flex container
- Add pt-[18vh] (or similar) to anchor content at ~30% from top
- Keep items-center for horizontal centering

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Hero content starts at approximately 28-32% from viewport top
2. Visual weight feels balanced, not bottom-heavy
3. npx vue-tsc --noEmit passes
