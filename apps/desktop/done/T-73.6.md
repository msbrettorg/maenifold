## Description
Add up/down arrow buttons to each step card in the timeline. Clicking up swaps the step with its predecessor, clicking down swaps with its successor. First step hides up button, last step hides down button. Step IDs remain immutable through reorder — only array position changes. Selection follows the moved step.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Down arrow button on each step (hidden on last step)
- [ ] Clicking up/down swaps step position in draftDefinition.steps
- [ ] Step IDs do not change during reorder
- [ ] Timeline re-renders with correct numbering after swap
- [ ] Selected step follows its new position
- [ ] isDirty becomes true after reordering
