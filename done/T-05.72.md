It is NOT a progress bar; it cannot be expressed in Tailwind classes (arbitrary px values per drag pixel) and has no semantic relationship to `<Progress>`. Replacing with shadcn Progress would be incorrect. Project's audit flags `<progress>`/`role="progressbar"`, neither of which is present.
